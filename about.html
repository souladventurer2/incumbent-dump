The mission.

One sentence: do not re-elect any current federal incumbent, regardless of party, until a real generational reset has happened.

Three principles.

1. Genuinely nonpartisan.

We do not endorse Democrats. We do not endorse Republicans. We do not endorse independents. We endorse turnover. If both parties' incumbents lose every cycle, both parties learn the same lesson at the same speed. That symmetry is the whole point.

2. Generational accountability.

The people who built the systems we're living under should not be the people maintaining them after they're underwater. Climate, debt, housing, entitlements â the bill comes due decades after the votes are cast. The people writing those bills should expect to be alive when they come due.

3. Stop confusing the seat with the person.

"But my representative is one of the good ones" is the sentence that has kept Congress at 14% approval and 95% re-election for decades. The institution is the problem. Replacing the institution starts with refusing to send any individual member back.

What this is not.

Not a PAC. Not a campaign. Not a party. Not a candidate. Not aligned with any movement, donor, or organization. The site is run by an unincorporated group of citizens with one shared belief: incumbency itself is the problem we vote on.

We do not ask for money. We do not collect data we don't need. The only thing we ask is that you vote against your incumbent in every primary and every general, and tell people why.
