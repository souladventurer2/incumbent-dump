Don't re-elect anyone.

Not left. Not right. The same people have been in office for decades and the country keeps breaking. Incumbent Dump is a nonpartisan call to vote out every current officeholder and start over with a generation that will actually inherit the consequences.

Why this is the move.

Polling says Americans hate Congress and re-elect them anyway. Both parties run a protection racket for their own incumbents. The only lever ordinary voters control is the one they keep refusing to pull: refusing to send the same people back.

It's nonpartisan

If your team's rep loses, the other team's rep loses too. Across the board, every cycle, until the political class stops thinking the seat belongs to them.

It's the only thing they fear

Donations don't scare incumbents. Bad press doesn't scare them. Losing primaries and general elections is the only feedback the system actually responds to.

It re-shuffles the deck

New people. Younger people. People who will still be alive when the bill comes due on climate, debt, and entitlements. The ones supposed to inherit it should help write it.

It costs you nothing

No money. No marches. No convincing your uncle. Just one box, every primary and every general, for as long as it takes.
